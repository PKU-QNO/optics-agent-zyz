2.3.2 Multipole moments of representative meta-atoms
The advantages of the multipole analysis as introduced is outlined in more detail in the
following. This is done by discussing the scattering properties and excited multipole moments
of some selected meta-atoms. It is shown that the formalism is useful to understand the
excited resonances in meta-atoms as well as to develop new design rules to improve them
further.
