The overlap integrals of Eq. 2.28 are performed on a virtual sphere of radius a. For non
spherical meta-atoms the points of highest symmetry are chosen as the center of the virtual
sphere.
The obtained scattering coeﬃcients are independent on the radius a as long as
the virtual sphere fully encloses the scatterer.
Since the scattered ﬁeld was assumed to
be known, Eq. 2.28 yields the scattering coeﬃcients for an arbitrary meta-atom, which are
the multipole moments in spherical coordinates.
However, spherical multipole moments
are sometimes hard to interpret. Therefore, transformation rules into Cartesian multipole
moments are required that appear to be the natural choice to discuss the properties of most
of the meta-atoms.
