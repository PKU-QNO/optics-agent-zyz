2.2 Scattering by a single sphere

homogeneous, local, linear, and isotropic material described by εsph(ω), µsph(ω) embedded

single sphere [146, 147], or simply Mie theory. The Helmholtz equation (Eq. 2.7) is solved
by a separation of the Laplace operator in spherical coordinates (r, θ, ϕ).
